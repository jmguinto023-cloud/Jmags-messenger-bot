This page explains how you can ask JMAG's Appliances to delete the information our Messenger assistant has about you. Our Privacy Policy is here: https://jmags-messenger-bot.onrender.com/privacy.

How to request deletion

Choose either option:

  1. Message our Facebook Page. Open Messenger, go to your conversation with JMAG's Appliances, and send: "Please delete my data."
  2. Call or text us at 09386369374 (Smart) or 09267229506 (Globe). Tell us the name on your Facebook account so we can find your conversation. We may ask you to send a short message from your Messenger account to confirm it is you.

You do not need to give a reason, and there is no fee.

What we delete

From our assistant's server, we delete:

  • your name and Page-scoped ID (PSID) stored by the assistant;
  • the conversation history (your messages and our replies);
  • records of photos and videos you sent, and the links to them;
  • the location or address and other repair details you shared in the chat;
  • follow-up state and any other assistant record stored for that conversation.

Where Meta's tools allow us to, we will also delete the conversation from our Facebook Page inbox.

What may be kept: if you already booked or completed a repair with us, we may keep a basic job record (name, contact number, address, repair done and warranty) only as long as needed for your warranty and for legal or accounting requirements. We will tell you if this applies.

Messages in your own Messenger app are controlled by you and Meta. You can delete the conversation from your Messenger app at any time.

Timeframe

We will complete your deletion request within 30 days and confirm by Messenger or text message when it is done. Even without a request, our assistant automatically deletes conversation records after 90 days without activity. Photo and video review records are deleted after 60 days.

Removing the app from your Facebook account

Our assistant works through our Facebook Page, so you usually do not log in to it. If you see it listed in your Facebook settings and want to remove it:

  1. Open Facebook and go to Settings & privacy, then Settings.
  2. Open Apps and websites (on some versions it is under Your activity or Accounts Center).
  3. Find the app and choose Remove.

The exact menu names can change as Facebook updates its app. Removing the app does not delete data we already hold, so please also send us a deletion request as described above.

You can also stop all messages from our Page by blocking the Page in Messenger.
