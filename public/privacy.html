This Privacy Policy explains how JMAG's Appliances ("JMAG's", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, shares and deletes information when you send a message to the JMAG's Appliances Facebook Page on Messenger, including when our automated Messenger assistant replies to you.

1. Who we are

JMAG's Appliances is a home-service TV and appliance repair business in the Philippines. Our branches are in San Pablo City, Laguna and General Trias, Cavite.

2. Information we collect

When you message our Facebook Page, we receive the following through Meta's Messenger Platform:

  • Your name as shown on your Facebook profile, and any name you give us in the chat.
  • Your Page-scoped ID (PSID). This is a number Meta assigns to you for our Page only. It lets us reply to you in the right conversation. It is not your Facebook password or login.
  • Message contents: the text of the messages you send us and the replies we send you.
  • Photos and videos you send, for example a video of your TV showing the problem.
  • Location or address you choose to share, such as your city, barangay, address, landmark or a map pin, so we can confirm that our home service covers your area and plan the visit.
  • Other details you choose to give us for a repair, such as the appliance brand, size, model, age and problem, your contact number and your preferred schedule.

We do not ask for, and you should not send, payment card numbers, bank passwords or one-time codes (OTPs) in Messenger.

3. How we use your information

We use this information only to:

  • answer your repair inquiries;
  • give you price quotes and estimates;
  • book and arrange home-service repairs, including sending you our booking form;
  • let our technician and customer service staff review your photos or videos and continue the conversation with you; and
  • keep the conversation going smoothly (for example, so we do not ask you the same question twice).

We do not use your information for any unrelated purpose, and we do not sell it.

4. Automated replies

Some replies on our Page are written by an automated assistant that uses an artificial intelligence (AI) service. The assistant replies only to messages you send us. It follows our price list and service policies. A human staff member takes over the conversation for bookings that need confirmation, discounts, complaints, warranty concerns, and whenever you ask to talk to a person. If you ask whether you are talking to a bot, the assistant will say that it is an automated assistant.

5. How we share your information

We never sell, rent or trade your information. We share it only as follows:

  • Hosting provider: our assistant runs on servers operated by Render. Conversation data is stored and processed there so the assistant can work.
  • AI service provider: the text of the conversation is sent to Anthropic (Claude) only to generate the assistant's replies.
  • Meta Platforms: Messenger is a Meta service. Messages are delivered through Meta, and Meta's own Privacy Policy applies to your use of Facebook and Messenger.
  • Our staff and technicians: only the details needed to answer you and complete your repair.
  • When required by law: if we are legally required to disclose information to authorities.

These providers process the data on our behalf to provide the service. We do not allow them to sell it.

6. How long we keep your information

Conversation logs and related records (including your name, PSID, messages, links to photos or videos you sent, and details you shared) are stored on our server for a limited period and then deleted automatically. Our assistant deletes conversation records after 90 days without activity in the conversation. Some short-term records used by our technician to review photos and videos are kept for up to 60 days, then deleted.

Messages also remain in our Facebook Page inbox and in your own Messenger app, which are managed by Meta. If you ask us to delete your data, we will also delete the conversation from our Page inbox where Meta's tools allow us to.

If you book a repair, we may keep basic job records (such as your name, contact number, address, the repair done and the warranty) for as long as needed to honor your warranty and meet legal and accounting requirements, then delete them.

7. How we protect your information

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) between Meta, our server and our service providers. We verify that incoming messages really come from Meta, keep access keys secret, and limit access to staff who need it. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Your rights and choices

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you may ask to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to its processing. You can also ask to talk to a person instead of the automated assistant at any time.

9. How to request deletion of your data

You can ask us to delete your data at any time by:

  • sending a message to the JMAG's Appliances Facebook Page saying "Please delete my data"; or
  • calling or texting 09386369374 (Smart) or 09267229506 (Globe).

We will delete your conversation records from our server within 30 days of your request and confirm when it is done. Full instructions are on our Data Deletion page: https://jmags-messenger-bot.onrender.com/data-deletion.

10. Children

Our service is meant for adults arranging appliance repairs. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and change the effective date above.
